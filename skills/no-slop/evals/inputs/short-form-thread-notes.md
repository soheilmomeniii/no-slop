Topic: why most positioning decks fail.

Material I have:
- I reviewed 40 seed-stage decks last month for a fund I advise.
- 31 of them described the product before naming the customer.
- The 9 that didn't all raised. That is correlation, not proof, and I should say so.
- My line from an earlier post, verbatim: "A category you have to explain is a category you do not own."
- One counterexample: Stripe's early deck described the product first and it worked, because the customer was obvious from the product.

Voice: mine. lowercase, blunt, no hype, no thread emoji, no "let that sink in".
