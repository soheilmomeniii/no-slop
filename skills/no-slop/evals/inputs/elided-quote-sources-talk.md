# Transcript excerpt: Dana Reyes at the Founder Infra meetup, April 3

Moderator: You said something backstage about pricing pages that I want you to repeat on the
record.

Reyes: Sure. Pricing pages are positioning documents that happen to have numbers on them. If a
visitor can read your tiers and cannot tell who each one is for, the numbers were never the
problem.

Moderator: And on channels. You have a strong view there.

Reyes: I do. The channel you own beats the channel you rent. Rented reach reprices itself the
moment it works. We learned that the expensive way, twice, before moving the newsletter onto
our own list.
