# On Competition

Most founders believe competition validates a market. They are wrong, and the error is expensive.

A crowded market signals that the opportunity has been recognized. Recognition attracts capital. Capital compresses margin. The founder who celebrates finding fifteen competitors has celebrated finding fifteen claims on the same revenue.

Monopoly is the only condition under which a business keeps what it earns. Everything else is a race toward zero profit, run by people who mistake activity for progress.
