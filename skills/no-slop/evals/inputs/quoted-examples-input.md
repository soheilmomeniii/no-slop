# What AI writing actually sounds like

Every AI-written blog post opens the same way. "In today's rapidly evolving landscape," it begins, before explaining that "this article explores" whatever it was asked about. Then it hedges: "it is important to note that results may vary."

The tell isn't any one phrase. It's the accumulation. A human might write "in short" once. A model writes it after every section, because it was trained to be complete.

I ran an experiment. I asked four models to write about supply chains and counted the tics. Every one of them used the phrase "not just X, but Y" at least twice. Three of them ended with "the future looks bright."

That last one is the giveaway. Nobody who has actually worked in logistics has ever ended anything with "the future looks bright."
