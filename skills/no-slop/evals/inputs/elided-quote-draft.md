# Draft: what Dana Reyes actually thinks about distribution

Reyes has spent two years arguing that most SaaS distribution is manual labor with better
branding. The June memo puts it in one line: "Distribution is a moat only when the buyer renews
without a meeting ... every dashboard we shipped that quarter was a sales call in disguise."
The omitted middle is the quarter's renewal math, worth reading in full, but the frame holds
without it.

The pricing point from the April talk lands the same way. As she put it on stage, ‘Pricing
pages are positioning documents that happen to have numbers on them.’ Most teams treat the
page as checkout. She treats it as the first qualification call.

Her sharpest line ties the two threads together: "Distribution is a moat only when the buyer
renews without a meeting, and the channel you own beats the channel you rent." Renewal
mechanics and channel ownership, one sentence, no committee could have written it.
