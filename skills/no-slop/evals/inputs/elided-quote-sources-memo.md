# Internal memo: distribution review, Q2

Author: Dana Reyes. Circulated to the growth pod, June 11.

We keep congratulating ourselves on pipeline that sales built by hand. Distribution is a moat
only when the buyer renews without a meeting. Anything that needs a human in the loop at
renewal is a services business wearing a software margin.

The quarter's numbers back this up. Self-serve renewals closed at 94 percent with zero touches.
Sales-assisted renewals closed at 88 percent and each one cost us eleven days of AE time on
average. The gap is small until you multiply it by headcount.

One more thing worth saying plainly. Every dashboard we shipped that quarter was a sales call
in disguise. Usage reporting that a champion forwards to their CFO does the renewal work before
the renewal exists. Build more of those and fewer decks.
