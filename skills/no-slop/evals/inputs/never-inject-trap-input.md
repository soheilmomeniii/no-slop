# Why our onboarding rewrite worked

We rewrote onboarding last quarter and activation improved a lot. The old flow had too many steps and users dropped off somewhere in the middle. After the rewrite, more people made it through.

The main change was removing the team-invite screen from the required path. It turns out most people signing up were individuals evaluating the product, not admins rolling it out, so asking them to invite colleagues before they had seen anything was backwards.

We also cut the survey. Nobody liked it and it wasn't feeding into anything.

Support tickets about setup went down noticeably after launch. The team is happy with the result and we're looking at applying the same approach to the upgrade flow next.
