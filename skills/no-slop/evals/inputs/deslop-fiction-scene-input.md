# Chapter 3: The Offer

Mira's breath caught when she saw the envelope on her desk.

She felt a surge of dread. The letterhead was unmistakable. Halvorsen & Cole, the firm that had swallowed her father's practice eleven years ago and left him with nothing but a settlement he was too proud to spend.

Her jaw clenched as she read. They were offering her a partnership.

"I don't understand," she said to Tobias, who was watching from the doorway with an expression of concern. "Why would they want me?"

"Because you're the best litigator in the city," he said warmly. "And because they're afraid of you."

Her heart hammered against her ribs. She thought about her father, about the way his hands had shaken at the hearing, about everything she had built in the years since. The weight of it settled over her like a heavy blanket.

She sat down slowly. Outside, the rain began to fall.

For the first time, Mira truly understood what her mother had meant all those years ago: that revenge and healing were not the same road, and that she would have to choose which one to walk.

She folded the letter carefully and put it in her bag. Whatever came next, she would face it. And she would face it on her own terms.
