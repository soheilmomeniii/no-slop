**Statement of changes.** The material was rewritten rather than copied: the taxonomy was
condensed to seven items, reframed as revision triggers inside this project's two-gate
structure, and three of the patterns were reduced to regular expressions that do not appear
in the original. No file from that project is redistributed here.

That project's separation of research-backed signals from community-observed ones, and its
insistence that word-level lists are editorial taste rather than authorship evidence,
directly shaped this skill's honesty rules. Credit where it is owed.
